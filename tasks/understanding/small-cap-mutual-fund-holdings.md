## Data-quality notes before curation

- The screenshots were captured by scrolling and some images overlap or skip rows. The tables record every visible row and remove obvious duplicates.
- Fund 1 and Fund 3 screenshots appear to have scroll gaps between the visible sections. Their tables should be reconciled against a complete holdings export before treating the list as exhaustive.
- Similar names are retained as separate entries when the fund lists them as separate securities, such as equity versus futures.
- The curated list should use normalized company names and should separately track: number of funds holding the stock, each fund’s weight, combined displayed weight, and the maximum single-fund weight.
