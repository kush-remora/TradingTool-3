# Adaptive Fresh Breakout Definition

The goal is to detect the first daily close above a meaningful consolidation ceiling without assuming that every stock absorbs supply for 20, 30, or 40 sessions. Breakout detection should model price structure only; volume, close strength, follow-through, and fake-breakout rejection remain separate confirmation stages. Phase 1 is a latest-date watchlist scanner, not a backtest.

The proposed direction is an ATR-adaptive structural resistance model replayed oldest-to-newest. A prior swing high becomes an active ceiling only after price meaningfully retreats from it; the ceiling includes a small volatility-based allowance. A fresh breakout occurs once, when today's close crosses above the active ceiling while yesterday's close was at or below it. The base duration is then an observed output—from ceiling formation to breakout—not a configured lookback.

The scanner must return every watchlist stock, its current state, latest completed candle, active ceiling, and raw candle-by-candle decisions for manual validation. The UI must also display the latest available Kite LTP and distinguish an intraday live breakout candidate from a completed-close breakout. Close-near-high, volume versus the prior 10-session average, and distance from the 52-week high are evidence-only fields for now and must not affect the core decision.

An uninterrupted rebound such as `100 → 80 → 81 → 82 → 83 → 84 → 85` has no post-floor rejection and therefore no local ceiling. Below two ATR it is `NO_CEILING`; at two ATR or more it is `STRONG_REBOUND`. Neither state is a breakout, and the scanner must never invent a ceiling merely to produce a signal.

Local-structure correction: a breakout remains a close above confirmed resistance; a two-ATR rise from a floor is momentum, not a breakout. The engine should confirm a local ceiling from a rejected peak followed by a later return into the same half-ATR area, then emit a fresh breakout only on the first completed close above that local upper boundary. A distant older ceiling is retained as major overhead context rather than blocking the local signal. If price rises at least two ATR from the active floor before any local ceiling is confirmed, report `STRONG_REBOUND` instead of inventing a breakout.
