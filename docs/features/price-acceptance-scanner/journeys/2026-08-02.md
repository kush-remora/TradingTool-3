# Price Acceptance Scanner — 2026-08-02

## Why it was built

We wanted a simple first-pass scanner for possible price acceptance around the latest open-close candle body. Repeated historical closes in that body can identify a price zone worth reviewing for a developing Wyckoff cause, without presenting the raw metric as a confirmed accumulation signal.

## What was implemented

- Added a pure backend engine that counts prior closes inside the anchor candle body over 20, 40, 60, 80, and 100 sessions.
- Excluded the anchor candle from every count and used the latest available candle on or before the requested as-of date.
- Added universe resolution through active `index_constituents` rows and daily candles through the existing cache/Kite path.
- Added `GET /api/strategy/price-acceptance/universes` and `GET /api/strategy/price-acceptance/scan`.
- Added a compact frontend screen with universe selection, as-of date, raw counts, hit rates, and usable-history visibility.

## Key decisions and tradeoffs

- No new table or migration: this is a derived, read-only diagnostic.
- The candle body uses `min(open, close)` through `max(open, close)`, not the full high-low range.
- Partial histories remain visible and report their available prior-session count; they are not silently treated as full 100-session samples.
- Ranking is by 100-session hit count only as a review aid. The scanner does not label rows as accumulation or create a trade signal.
