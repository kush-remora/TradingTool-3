# Weekly Low Limit Backtest

## Why

Kush wanted a small, falsifiable six-month experiment: place a limit buy 1% above the previous completed week's low during the following week, then test a fixed 5% target and 5% stop. This is a price-only mean-reversion baseline and is not presented as Wyckoff accumulation evidence.

## Implemented

- Added a Kotlin backtest engine using daily OHLC candles only.
- Added the `POST /api/strategy/weekly-low-limit-backtest/run` endpoint.
- Added single-stock and existing-watchlist selection modes.
- Added six-month lookback with a two-week data buffer for previous-week lows.
- Added selectable rule variants: any-day entry with a maximum five-trading-day hold, or Monday–Wednesday entry with a Friday time exit.
- Added next-week limit orders at 1% above the previous week's low, adverse gap fills at the open, +5% target, -5% stop, conservative stop-first ambiguity handling, and `POSITION_OPEN_SKIP` records when the five-day variant would overlap an active position.
- Added a pre-market filter: if the previous week's final close is below the adjusted limit, no following-week order is placed. These setups are recorded as `PREMARKET_FILTER_SKIP` and counted separately from no-fills.
- Added a 1% open-deviation filter: when a candidate entry candle touches the limit, the setup is skipped if its open is more than 1% above or below the limit. These setups are recorded as `OPEN_DEVIATION_SKIP`.
- Added the exact previous-week-low date to the audit output, including its weekday in the UI.
- Added a per-trade `Validate daily path` page showing low, open, close, high, and close-to-close daily percentage change from the previous low through the entry week and five forward trading sessions.
- Added a compact audit table showing every setup, including no-fills.

## Decisions

- The current incomplete calendar week is excluded.
- Exits are evaluated from the trading day after entry because daily OHLC cannot establish intraday order sequencing on the entry candle.
- The five-day variant counts available daily candles, not calendar days, and can carry across a week boundary.
- Watchlist results are independent per symbol; portfolio allocation, brokerage, slippage, and VWAP are out of scope for V1.
