# Base Rhythm Analysis

## Why This Was Built

Strict 20-session shape classification is valuable as a mathematical eligibility gate, but it does not by itself explain the 60-session Wyckoff progression a trader sees on a chart. KALYANKJIL highlighted this: its earlier 20-session window was not a Golden Flat, while its later price action is easier to understand as a sequence of repair, contraction, test, and expansion.

## What Was Implemented

- Added a forward-safe 60-session Base Rhythm diagnostic to every accumulation timeline snapshot.
- Split the preceding 60 trading sessions into six chronological 10-session blocks.
- Each block stores:
  - price direction: falling, flat, or rising
  - range state: contracting, steady, or expanding versus the prior block
  - volume state: contracting, steady, or expanding versus the prior block
- Added a compact `Base rhythm` timeline column with hover details for each block.
- Kept Golden Flat, valid/invalid decisions, and ranking behavior unchanged.

## Key Decisions

- Price moves within ±3% over a 10-session block are treated as flat.
- A range or average-volume change of at least 20% from the prior block is marked contracting or expanding.
- Thresholds live in `config/accumulation_analysis_config.json` for simple future calibration.
- Bumped the analysis algorithm version to `v9-base-rhythm`, making prior runs visibly stale rather than silently rendering missing rhythm data.
