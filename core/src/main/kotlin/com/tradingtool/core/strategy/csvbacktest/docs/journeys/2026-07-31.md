# Breakout Close Reclaim Entry

## Why

The CSV backtester needs to model a delayed post-breakout entry: wait for price to reclaim the breakout day's close after an expected pullback instead of automatically buying the next day.

## Implemented

Added the `BREAKOUT_CLOSE_RECLAIM` entry strategy. It inspects the 30 trading sessions after the signal day, finds the first close strictly above the signal day's close, and buys at the next trading session's open. A signal is rejected when no reclaim close appears in those 30 sessions or when no next session exists for the fill.

## Decisions

The 30-session limit applies to the confirmation close, not the subsequent fill session. Therefore a reclaim on session 30 validly enters on session 31. The limit is fixed because it is the requested rule, avoiding an unnecessary per-run setting.

## Validation

Focused evaluator tests cover the normal delayed entry, the session-30 boundary, and rejection after the window. Frontend tests and the production build verify the new selectable rule compiles with the existing page.

## Follow-up

Compare the new rule's outcomes against next-day-open and retest entries on the same signal batches.
