# Two-Day Green Candle Backtest

## Why it was built

Kush wanted a deliberately simple momentum backtest to observe whether two consecutive green days with more than a 1% close-to-close gain tend to continue toward a 5% target.

## What was implemented

- Watchlist selection through the existing index/watchlist options.
- Latest 40 trading-session test window.
- Entry at the third day open after two qualifying setup candles.
- 5% target based on the entry open, triggered by a later daily high.
- Per-trade observations for volume, daily move, open-to-close movement, low-to-high range, and close location.
- Explicit indicators for rising setup volume and rising setup move; these are observations, not filters.
- Frontend audit table with expandable three-day candle details.

## Decisions and trade-offs

The strategy evaluates every qualifying signal, so overlapping signals are retained for observation. Trades without a target hit by the end of available data remain unresolved instead of receiving an invented exit. Buy-day OHLCV is displayed only after the session because it is not known at the open.
