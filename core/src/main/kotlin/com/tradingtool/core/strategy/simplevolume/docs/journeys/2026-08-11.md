# Simple volume / pocket-pivot chart signals

## Why

The compact stock review needed a backend-owned interpretation of the TradingView Simple Volume with Pocket Pivots indicator so volume colors and markers are consistent across the console and future scanners.

## Implemented

- Added a pure Kotlin classifier for pocket pivots, high-volume up/down bars, dry volume, Bull Snort bars, and warm-up sessions.
- Used completed-session baselines: 50 sessions for the volume average and 10 sessions for the pocket-pivot comparison.
- Added per-day signal fields to the stock-detail response, including relative volume, baseline volume, pocket-pivot, and Bull Snort flags.
- Updated the compact `lightweight-charts` pane with signal colors, Bull Snort markers, readout values, and compact visibility toggles.

## Decisions / tradeoffs

The backend uses the prior 50 completed sessions rather than including the current candle in its own average. This avoids self-dilution while a live candle is forming and keeps the calculation explicit. The logic is a price/volume classification, not true order-flow delta and not a trade recommendation.
