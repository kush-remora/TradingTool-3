# Weekly Floor Rebound Backtest v1

## Why

Test the hypothesis that a stock with three tightly aligned weekly lows, a prior decline, and a recovery through the floor can produce a positive one-week swing. This is a price-structure experiment, not a claim of confirmed Wyckoff accumulation.

## Delivered

- Added a Kotlin weekly-floor rebound engine and candle-loading service.
- Added `POST /api/strategy/weekly-floor-rebound/backtest` for a selected NSE equity.
- Added a searchable Kite-instrument UI page with NETWEB as the default.
- The report covers the latest 200 completed trading sessions, with 252 earlier sessions used only for 52-week context.
- Trade rows include rejected/no-entry weeks as well as fills, exits, gross returns, and gap/ambiguity flags.

## Rules and Tradeoffs

- Floor: the three immediately prior completed ISO weeks must have lows within 2%.
- Entry: first trading day reaches 1% above the floor; a gap fills at the opening price.
- Stop: 0.5% below the floor. Target: 5% above actual entry. Unresolved trades exit at the final close of that week.
- Daily bars that hit both stop and target exit at the stop. A stop gap exits at the opening price.
- V1 deliberately reports gross returns only. Charges and slippage are deferred until the raw price hypothesis has earned that complexity.
